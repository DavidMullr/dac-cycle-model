The same credits apply wherever an input is repeated, including regression fixtures, source evidence and the material packages embedded in all 16 completed exports.

## COF source data

Both humid cases use 400 ppm CO₂ capacities at 0%, 25%, 50% and 75% relative humidity. For water uptake, the COF-999 case uses 23 isotherm nodes from Fig. 3c, and the COF-1000 case uses a reported value of 0.07 g water per g sorbent at 50% relative humidity. The COF-1000 kinetic parameters come from a team fit to the dry uptake trace in Fig. 3b.

The material records identify the source data and the original measurement conditions. The repository distributes the selected numerical series in Carbon Team’s own input and test formats. The original publisher workbooks, article text and figures are available at the source. Swiss guidance distinguishes published scientific facts from the authors’ expression and from original compilations. Springer Nature says it claims no intellectual property rights in research datasets associated with its papers. We could not verify a separate licence from the data owners for redistribution of the selected COF series as compilations. [Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property](https://www.ige.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/schuetzen/urheberrecht/e/Public_Domain_Fact_Sheet_EN_04.2020.pdf) · [Springer Nature data policy](https://support.nature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000237622-select-the-right-licence-for-your-data)
