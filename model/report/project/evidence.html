A closed mass balance tells us that the model accounts for the CO₂ entering and leaving the bed. To judge the predicted daily capture, we also need uptake, desorption and collection measurements at the intended operating conditions.

Material evidence

Lewatit has a multi-temperature property and humidity-model basis, but the hot humid cycle remains conditional. The saved 116°C request and local temperature around 120°C are outside what should be assumed acceptable for the planned prototype without checking the actual material limits.

For COF-1000 and COF-999, the fresh protocol analysis withheld chronological tails, a whole humidity condition and a temperature condition. Both retained failed RH75 capacity checks. COF-999 also retained a failed 100°C temperature-extrapolation check. A passing temporal fit is not proof that an empirical rate transfers to CO₂-rich vacuum regeneration.

The HOB temperature-shift representation needs further work. Source-curve irregularities and the large hot PVDF loading discrepancy remain visible. A replacement must use consistent specimen data and connect equilibrium temperature dependence to adsorption energy; a better loading fit alone is insufficient.

TEPA, MOF-808 derivatives, MIL/PEI and the other humid scenarios have differing combinations of measured unary data, protocol fits and transferred water or kinetic assumptions. These differences limit cross-material interpretation even with the same operating case.

Evidence for the resin and regeneration studies

Purolite A110. Water-presaturated isotherms at 25 °C cover dry, 30%, 55% and 80% RH. The humid curves begin above atmospheric CO₂ partial pressure. The model extends them to the feed concentration and uses a temperature shift for hot desorption. Dry uptake rates and measured relative diffusivities help constrain adsorption. Hot wet desorption uses the same rate law in reverse. In a matched 48-hour run at 65% RH A110 collected 2.58 kg and Lewatit 5.14 kg. A dry isotherm comparison at imposed endpoints answers a different question because it omits bed heat transfer and collection losses. Material law · Paired runs and solver identities

Direct steam. Generator input, injected steam, liquid left in the bed, bed drainage and knockout condensate have separate meters. The one-hour preset starts with CO₂ on the resin. In that test, product can exceed net uptake from air as the initial inventory falls. The 48-hour timing study starts clean and dry. Its steam cases use fixed-weather cycle reuse. The controlling uncertainties are the wet equilibrium at high humidity, the rate of condensation and evaporation, and delivered steam flow against backpressure. We need simultaneous outlet CO₂ and moisture, bed temperatures and separate drain weights to test that calculation. Steam checks and sensitivities

Lewatit aging. Hunt et al. measured 68.8% retained capacity after 180 cycles at their operating conditions. The team simulated four fixed capacities on one electrical cycle. At the lowest capacity the model still collected 4.66 kg in 48 h, compared with 5.52 kg fresh. Linear and exponential scenarios connect the fresh and published endpoints. The loss rate for our bed, its response to steam and its service life remain open. A useful first comparison is CO₂ uptake on fresh and cycled beads at the same temperature, humidity, concentration and conditioning history. Cycling study · Model inputs and results

Current result boundary

The review dataset contains ten humid baseline results, one matched dry Lewatit result and five completed desorption-rate sensitivities. HOB/PVDF and COF-999 sensitivities are incomplete after worker exits. Their partial calculations are not performance estimates.

The ten archived humid baseline cases match after removing labels. Executable source releases remain identified per case. CALF-20 and COF-1000 use the accepted re-audits of their existing results, with the original trace-coverage audit failures retained in the evidence record. A re-audit changes the accounting verdict, not the simulated output.

Model output uncertainty has not been quantified. Source uncertainty, parameter uncertainty, model discrepancy and numerical error must remain separate. Neither material-family spread nor a successful conservation check is a confidence interval.

Validation needed before a prototype prediction

Measure shaped-material adsorption/desorption under the intended humid cycle, including hot states and repeated operation. Check bed pressure drop and packing, internal temperature gradients and heater transfer, leaks and delivered pump flow. Then compare complete-cycle product and electrical input against the model. Refit only on declared training data and reserve independent conditions for testing.

The software verification record is in Review checks. It states which portable checks ran, what still needs an independent computer or physical measurement.
