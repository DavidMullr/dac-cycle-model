# Data needed for the small prototype

The working basis is Lewatit VP OC 1065, resistance wire inside the bed and the KNF N922 stainless-steel IP54 pump family. Keep the current screening case as a reference; enter the prototype as its own case.

| Needed input or measurement | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Chosen dimensions, flow direction, dry mass and open flow areas | Defines bed volume, velocity, heat paths and pressure loss |
| Actual material batch, conditioning, bead-size distribution, packed density and voidage | Fixes the dry shaped basis and hydraulic/thermal properties |
| Material temperature limit and cycling stability under the intended atmosphere | Sets a defensible operating envelope |
| Wire position, electrical power, insulation/contact and several internal temperatures | Tests heater-to-bed transfer and local hot spots |
| Pressure drop versus airflow, including filters, screens and heater | Defines the fan requirement at load |
| Pump variant and flow at simultaneous inlet vacuum and outlet pressure | Tests whether the intended collection/delivery duty is achievable |
| Leak rate and line/valve conductance | Determines achieved vacuum and dilution |
| Humid uptake and hot desorption on the actual shaped material | Tests equilibrium and kinetic assumptions |
| Product amount, wet/dry CO₂ fraction, condensate and electrical input per cycle | Supports independent mass/energy and performance validation |
| Downstream flow, duty and wet/dry concentration basis | Completes the approximately 1% CO₂, 3 bar absolute interface |
| Component quotes, installation and operating loads | Completes CAPEX and operating cost |

The fan has not been selected. Its free-air maximum is not the required operating point: use the total system pressure at the chosen flow. A temperature target or pump name alone is not a validated device specification.

Prioritize geometry and material limits, then pressure-flow and heater measurements. Collect independent cycles or operating conditions for validation rather than fitting and evaluating on the same data.
